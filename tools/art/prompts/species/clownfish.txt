An ocellaris clownfish. A short deep oval body, tall at the shoulder and tapering to a
narrow base of the tail. Bright saturated orange. Three vertical white bands, each one
edged in black on both sides: one just behind the eye running down across the gill, one
across the middle of the body bulging forward at its centre, one near the base of the
tail. Rounded fan-shaped tail fin. A rounded dorsal fin along the top edge and rounded
pectoral fin on the flank, both orange with black margins. One large round eye with a
black pupil and a white catchlight.
