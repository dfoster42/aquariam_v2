A maroon anemonefish. A deep oval body, slightly taller and rounder than a common
clownfish. Deep burgundy red shading to a darker plum along the underside. A single
narrow white band directly behind the eye, running from the top of the head down across
the gill. Rounded tail fin, rounded dorsal fin and rounded pectoral fin, all the same
burgundy with dark margins. One large round eye with a black pupil and a white
catchlight.
